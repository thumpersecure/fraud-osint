Forensic techniques

  • Forensic Investigation Planning — engagement methodology from allegation response to reporting, with the fraud theory approach, privilege and Three Lines Model.
  • Bank Statement and Cash Analysis — proof of cash, kiting, lapping, check tampering and the expenditures method as step-by-step tests.
  • Journal Entry and Ledger Testing — extracting and testing general-ledger entries with SQL, Excel, Python, Benford and sampling.
  • Link and Network Analysis — entity resolution, fuzzy matching, link charts and shell-vendor detection.
  • Revenue and Earnings Manipulation Tests — Beneish M-Score, Altman Z, cut-off, channel stuffing, bill-and-hold and reserve tests with worked examples.
  • Forensic Interviewing and Statement Analysis — cognitive interview, PEACE, SUE, admission-seeking, Upjohn and deception-detection limits.
  • Business Valuation and Damages Quantification — DCF, lost-profit and but-for models, discount rates and valuation standards with a worked example.
  • Litigation Support and Damages — Expert roles, lost-profits and fraud-loss quantification, tracing rules and prejudgment remedies.
  • Hidden Assets in Divorce and Bankruptcy — lifestyle analysis, tracing, fraudulent-transfer law, bankruptcy fraud and trustee techniques.

Fraud schemes and the people behind them

  • Ponzi and Investment Fraud — Ponzi, pyramid, affinity and pig-butchering schemes; Madoff and Stanford; tracing, LIBR and clawbacks.
  • Procurement and Contract Fraud — Bid rigging, kickbacks, change-order abuse and shell vendors; bid screens and vendor-master analytics.
  • Healthcare and Insurance Fraud — Upcoding, AKS and Stark, DOJ takedowns, claims analytics and statistical sampling.
  • Payment Fraud and BEC — Business email compromise, wire recall and the Financial Fraud Kill Chain, check fraud, romance, APP and deepfake fraud.
  • Tax Fraud and Evasion — criminal tax offenses, indirect proof methods, FBAR/FATCA, employment-tax and refund fraud.
  • Public Sector Fraud and Improper Payments — GAO estimates, PPP/EIDL and unemployment fraud, PRAC, Do Not Pay and data-matching techniques.
  • Fraudster Psychology and the Dark Triad — perpetrator profile from ACFE data, Dark Triad research, behavioral red flags and the limits of profiling.
  • Financial Fraud Case Studies in Detection — eight regulator-documented accounting frauds analysed by red flags, detection route and forensic technique.

  • Whistleblowers and the False Claims Act — qui tam, SEC/DOJ programs, retaliation law and tip corroboration.
  • Sanctions and Export Controls — OFAC, the 50 percent rules, BIS, evasion typologies and screening methods.
  • Shell Companies and Beneficial Ownership — shell and front companies, nominees, registers (US CTA 2026, UK, EU) and how to pierce them.
